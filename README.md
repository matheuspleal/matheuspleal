### About me
🇺🇸 I'm a Full Stack engineer specializing in backend and software architecture, working with JavaScript and TypeScript since 2016. My strength is large-scale microservices, with event-driven architecture and distributed consistency patterns such as Saga and Transactional Outbox. Today I'm a Senior Software Engineer at iFood, where I also work with Kotlin and Spring Boot. What drives me is seeing technology, process and people come together to transform a business.

I started as an entrepreneur. In 2016 I co-founded Working Digital, a digital marketing agency that served more than 500 clients, and as CTO I led the technology team and the architecture of what we built. Running a company taught me what still guides my engineering: every technical decision is also a business decision, every trade-off needs an owner, and good architecture is the one your team can sustain. In 2021 I handed the agency over and moved fully into engineering.

I spent over three years at Superlógica, where I was promoted to Staff Software Engineer and became a technical reference for the engineers in the Housing business unit. I led the architecture of a NestJS Backend for Frontend that became the integration layer for auth, back-office, mobile and web, and designed outbox-relay to solve the dual write problem between the database and Kafka. At iFood, I've replaced a legacy service with a new one built on a redesigned data model.

How I work: I start from system design and trade-off analysis, and I write every decision down in an ADR. I'm used to distributed environments with dozens of services, structured with Clean Architecture and DDD, and I treat observability as part of the design: metrics, traces and logs that tell me whether a change does what we expected. With AI, the harness is engineering too: Claude Code with skills, MCP servers, read-only credentials and human review. It makes me faster, and the bottleneck has moved to code review and cognitive load.

I care about people as much as systems. I've mentored engineers, including one who earned a promotion from mid-level to senior, and I believe good engineering culture comes from clear processes that help people instead of slowing them down.

Alongside work, I earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science at UNESP, with research published at IEEE LATINCOM 2024, and I'm finishing an MBA in Software Architecture.

Core technologies: JavaScript · TypeScript · Kotlin · Node.js · NestJS · Spring Boot · React · React Native · PostgreSQL · DynamoDB · Apache Kafka · AWS · Kubernetes · Datadog
